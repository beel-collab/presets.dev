# Rails sources

Fetched 12 November 2026.

- https://rubyonrails.org/2026/11/4/Rails-8.0.5.2-and-8.1.4.1-have-been-released is the 4 November 2026 security release. 8.1.4.1 is the 8.1 security line.
- https://rubyonrails.org/2025/10/22/rails-8-1 describes Active Job continuations as resume-from-the-last-completed-step, not as an idempotency key.
- https://guides.rubyonrails.org/active_job_basics.html documents GlobalID: passing an Active Record object stores the class and id.
- https://guides.rubyonrails.org/upgrading_ruby_on_rails.html says Rails 8.1 requires Ruby 3.2.0 or newer.
- https://guides.rubyonrails.org/v6.0/6_0_release_notes.html deprecates `update_attributes` in favor of `update`. https://guides.rubyonrails.org/v8.0.0/6_1_release_notes.html records the removal. https://guides.rubyonrails.org/8_1_release_notes.html deprecates an order-dependent finder such as `first` without an `order`.
